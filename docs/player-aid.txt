Turn Summary:

OBSERVE

1. Play a card and move matching scientists
2. If the vehicle matches, move the flag
3a. (optional) Pay 1 <VP> to "refresh" the pool of available cards.
3b. Take 1 of the available cards or the top card from the deck.

or REGROUP

1. Take 1 <VP> for each scientist in your camps.
2. Discard 0-4 cards and draw that many cards from the deck.
3. Move all your scientists from both camps to a single location.
